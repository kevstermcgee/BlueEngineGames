# Pulse Nova Status

## Completed
- Fast-paced 3D kinetic arena shooter simulation built on BlueEngine's `custom-sim` architecture.
- 5 escalating waves of enemies (Scout Drones, Sentinel Spheres, Heavy Goliaths) with pathing and ranged attacks.
- High-velocity pulse blaster, kinetic dash, jumping, and 4 arena launch pads with upward impulse physics.
- Chain-reaction explosions, combo multiplier system, and floating energy shard pickups.
- Procedural audio soundscape using 11 synthesised SFX presets.
- 3D visual presentation: custom neon cyber colosseum, glowing pillars, dais, animated bobbing crystals, muzzle flashes, spark bursts, and shockwave rings.
- 2D HUD: dynamic crosshair, shield integrity meter, dash recharge prompt, wave banner, score, combo multiplier, and threat radar.
- Full save state persistence (F5/F9) with validated determinism.
- Identity, icon set, packaging (`dist/`), desktop shortcut (`Pulse Nova.lnk`), and verified ship gate.
- Integrated into `BlueEngineGames` catalog, releases, and multi-game shortcut installer.
