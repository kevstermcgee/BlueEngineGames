# Slapstick

Two-player air hockey: defend your goal and be first to score seven times.
The local preview includes an AI opponent. Online play uses the separate server
described in docs/HOSTING.md. Move your paddle with the mouse or left gamepad stick.
Esc opens the menu; F5/F9 save/load in solo preview.
Music and effects can be adjusted in Settings.

This is a playtest: the rules, save states and networking have automated checks;
human network gameplay and tuning have not been verified.
